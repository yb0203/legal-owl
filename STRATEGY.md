# Legal Owl Strategy

## Target problem

Solo lawyers and small law firms waste significant time on manual, disconnected workflows — monitoring e-courts for case status, maintaining Excel-based case records, reading through large volumes of documents, and sending client update emails manually. Everything is siloed across court portals, spreadsheets, and email, with no intelligent layer connecting them, leaving lawyers acting as data entry clerks instead of doing legal work.

## Our approach

Legal Owl pairs workflow automation with case-grounded AI — so every answer, draft, or suggestion is traceable to real case data and legal domain knowledge, not generic AI output. This makes the AI trustworthy enough to act on, moving lawyers from "what happened" to "what do I do next" — something passive case management tools cannot offer.

## Who it's for

**Primary:** Solo lawyers and small law firms managing multiple active cases — they're hiring Legal Owl to eliminate the manual overhead of court monitoring, document review, and client updates so they can spend their time on actual legal work.

## Key metrics

- **Average cases per lawyer per month** — case count per active user; tracked in product dashboard
- **Hours spent per case** — estimated from activity data; should decrease as automation kicks in
- **Client update automation rate** — percentage of client communications sent by the system vs typed manually
- **AI chat engagement per active case** — number of AI assistant queries per case per week; drops to zero if lawyers stop trusting or using it
- **Time-to-case-status-update** — hours between a court event and the update reflecting in the platform

## Tracks

### Court monitoring

Automated tracking of e-courts for case status, judgements, and hearing dates — replacing manual portal checks.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Eliminates the highest-frequency manual task so lawyers don't have to act as court watchers.

### Case management

Structured, automatically updated case records replacing Excel — status, remarks, history, and next steps in one place.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The workflow foundation that connects all other tracks; automation has nowhere to land without it.

### AI document assistant

Reads, ingests, and summarizes case documents; provides a chat interface grounded in specific case files and legal domain knowledge.

_Why it serves the approach:_ The core AI bet — grounding responses in real case data is what makes the assistant trustworthy enough to act on, unlike generic AI tools.

### Client experience

Automated client update emails and a client-facing dashboard where clients can view the live status of all their cases with the firm.

_Why it serves the approach:_ Closes the loop on manual client communication and gives clients self-serve visibility, reducing inbound queries back to the lawyer.
